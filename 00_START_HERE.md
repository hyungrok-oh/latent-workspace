# Start Here

This workspace is for studying Anthropic research papers and turning the notes into an English technical blog with an LLM-agent knowledge graph.

## Current Priority

1. Read and summarize Anthropic research papers.
2. Store each paper note in a structured format.
3. Extract concepts, methods, risks, and relationships into a knowledge graph.
4. Convert selected notes into polished English blog posts.
5. Build the blog after the first 2-3 notes exist.

## First Study Targets

1. Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models
   - Track: Interpretability
   - URL: https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/index.html
   - Why first: this is the current anchor paper for studying verbalizable internal representations, J-space/J-lens-style analysis, and the connection between internal model state and reportable reasoning.

2. Natural Language Autoencoders
   - Track: Interpretability
   - URL: https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/nla/index.html
   - Why second: this appears to be the natural follow-up for translating or structuring internal representations in language-like form.

3. Tracing the thoughts of a large language model
   - Track: Interpretability / Safety
   - URL: https://www.anthropic.com/research/tracing-thoughts-language-model

4. Agentic misalignment: How LLMs could be insider threats
   - Track: Agent Architecture / Safety
   - URL: https://www.anthropic.com/research/agentic-misalignment

5. Constitutional AI: Harmlessness from AI Feedback
   - Track: Safety
   - URL: https://www.anthropic.com/research/constitutional-ai-harmlessness-from-ai-feedback

## Workflow

For each paper:

1. Create a research note from `templates/paper-note.md`.
2. Fill in metadata, claims, method, evidence, limitations, and follow-up questions.
3. Add graph nodes and edges to the note.
4. Decide whether the note is ready to become a blog post.
5. Convert it into a polished English post.

## Blog Timing

Do not build the full blog first. First create at least:

- 3 paper notes
- 1 concept glossary
- 1 knowledge graph seed file
- 1 polished blog post draft

After that, build the blog around real content instead of guessing the structure.
